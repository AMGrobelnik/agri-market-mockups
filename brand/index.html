Names

Recommended

Thresh

Separate the signal from the chaff.

Threshing plus threshold: the backtest keeps world models that predicted reality and blows the rest away. One syllable, obvious ticker ($THRESH). No crypto, agri or prediction-market collision. Free: thresh.markets, thresh.so, threshprotocol.xyz.

Backup

Sirocco

Know which way the hot wind blows.

The Saharan wind that scorches Mediterranean olive and wheat: reads at once as European climate stress. No collisions. sirocco.markets and sirocco.fun are available.

Clashes

Hedgerow

Hedge the harvest.

Hedge plus crop rows; fits the Resilience Fund. But Hedgehog Markets is an established Solana prediction market, so a Solana engineer would confuse the two.

Option

Foresow

Foresee it. Sow it. Settle it.

Foresaw plus sow. No collisions found, but the pun needs saying out loud once. github.com/foresow and foresow.com are taken.

Option

Oddsfield

Every field has odds.

Odds over a field: says prediction market for crops in one word. Literal and less flashy; oddsfield.com is taken.
